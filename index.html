About

I have spent 10+ years in demand planning, allocation and pricing, from a 35-country international business at LC Waikiki to a 62-store retail group. That is where I learned where forecasts break, and where human judgment has to stay in charge: markdown depth, an export deal, what to do with 40,000 units of excess stock. I build AI around those points: approval-gated agents, and measurement of forecast error, bias and FVA. I work beside the team that owns the problem and build the solution where it runs.

What I do

Agent and workflow automation. I design multi-agent systems and automated workflows (Claude Code) with clear roles, lighter models for routine steps and a reviewer step before anything leaves the system.

AI for planning and forecasting. Time-series forecasting with error, bias and Forecast Value Added (FVA) measurement, so a team sees what each step of its forecasting process contributes.

Human-in-the-loop governance and EU compliance. Evidence trails and checkpoints where a person decides; working on AI-assisted CBAM and EmpCo (EU green claims) workflows.

How I work

My delivery approach: work beside the team.

  1. Listen on site. I sit with the people who own the problem and map how decisions are made today.
  2. Find the break. I locate where forecasts and data fail and which decisions need a human.
  3. Build in place. I build the workflow inside the existing tools and routines.
  4. Run and measure. I run it with the team, track error and value added, and adjust.

Selected AI Work

Each case: problem, what I built, stage.

1. SENSE: a multi-agent personal assistant

  • Problem: Working with AI costs time because context has to be explained again every session, and unattended agents can act without approval.
  • What I built: A multi-agent system on Claude Code. A main agent routes each request to a specialist subagent (career, EU compliance, business ideas); a separate reviewer agent checks every output before delivery. One rulebook governs all agents; nothing is deleted or sent without human approval; routine tasks go to lighter models.
  • Stack: Claude Code, Markdown rule files, GitHub.
  • Status: Running; I use it for my own work. The public repo holds the project description and rulebook; agent definitions and personal data stay private.
  • My role: Sole designer and builder.
  • Link: github.com/samettbakar/sense (README and rulebook, in Turkish)

2. Forecast error, bias and FVA measurement design

  • Problem: Planners override statistical forecasts often; without measurement, no one knows whether those overrides help.
  • What I built: A time-series forecasting skill, an automated monitoring step and an FVA table that measures model error and bias.
  • Stack: AI-assisted time-series forecasting, FVA method, anonymized womenswear data.
  • Status: Built; being applied to an anonymized womenswear case.
  • My role: Method design and build.

3. AI Markdown & Clearance Simulator design

  • Problem: Excess stock has four exits (sell, transfer, discount, export) and each one returns different cash.
  • What I built: A simulator that splits excess stock by SKU across those four routes and shows the cash each pricing scenario returns.
  • Stack: AI-assisted tool; the clearance logic comes from my own practice (see Brooks Brothers).
  • My role: Sole designer and builder.

4. EU compliance: CBAM data bridge and EmpCo green-claims audit design

  • Problem: EU importers now ask their non-EU suppliers, including Turkish exporters, for verifiable embedded-emissions data (CBAM definitive period since 1 Jan 2026), and environmental claims must be substantiated under EmpCo (Directive (EU) 2024/825, applies from 27 Sep 2026). Both need evidence work, not just advice.
  • What I built: Two service designs with AI-assisted workflows: for CBAM, facility data preparation and verifier-ready evidence files; for EmpCo, claim inventory, risk rating, rewrite suggestions and an evidence checklist. Neither gives legal opinions, verification or guarantees. Legal review stays outside scope.
  • Stack: AI-assisted workflows, official EU sources (European Commission, EUR-Lex).
  • Status: Design complete; market validation planned.
  • My role: Researcher and service designer; delivery planned on site, with the client's own data.

Also on GitHub: supply-chain-case holds two apparel strategy cases: a DTC womenswear strategy (Brand-X) and an operating-model strategy (TGIF).
